Finishes by voice engine check

Call out sail numbers; they are recorded, and finishes are made from the recording. Voice recordings are stored on this device.

The race

Calling finishers

Call out each sail number, then “now” as they finish, or press NOW: “sixteen thirteen … now”.

Any way you’d read it off the sail: “one six one three”, “sixteen thirteen”, “four oh seven eight”, “seven double one one”. A long sail number’s last three or four digits will do. Leave a pause between competitors. Everything said near the phone is recorded. One tone: a sail number was recognised. Two low tones: a sail number not on the entry list. A click: a time was marked.
